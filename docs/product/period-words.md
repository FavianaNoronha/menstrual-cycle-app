# What women call periods (and how the bot handles it)

Many women, especially on shared phones and in villages, never say "period". The bot must understand the words they actually use, and must never repeat the ones that carry shame.

## Rules
1. **Understand loosely, answer neutrally.** Whatever she writes, the read-back says "period started" in plain words in her language. This also catches wrong guesses, since she sees what we understood.
2. **Never echo stigma words.** Terms that mean "impure/untouchable" (e.g. Tamil *theettu*, Telugu *maila*, Kannada *mailige*) may be recognised in future, but the bot never says them, never agrees with the idea, and never tells her to stay away from anything or anyone.
3. **No bare ambiguous words.** "MC" (also an abuse in Hindi), "dates", and "dooram/thooram" (distance) are only matched in first-person phrases with a verb ("my mc started", "mera mahina aa gaya").
4. **Her phrase wins.** If she uses her own word every time, the bot should learn to treat it as hers (not built yet; needs consent text update).
