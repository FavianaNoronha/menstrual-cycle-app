# Our promise to users (public, and the bot says it on PROMISE)

1. **Tracking is free, forever.** Logging your period, pain and mood, predictions, the pain plan, and the doctor brief never go behind a paywall. Flo's biggest complaint is features moving to Premium; we commit not to do that to core tracking.
2. **Your data is yours.** EXPORT gives you everything as a table. DELETE erases it. Never sold, never used for ads, never shared with advertisers or data brokers.
3. **No fake certainty.** We show a range, say when we're unsure, and tell you when to see a doctor. We never diagnose and never give doses.
4. **Safe on a shared phone.** DISCREET mode; no pink, no "period" in anything we send on our own.
5. **How we earn money:** only from things you choose, like a doctor consult through a partner. Never from your data. If this ever changes we tell you first and you can export and leave.

**Before publishing:** the founder must confirm she can keep promise 1 and 5 financially (cost of WhatsApp templates, hosting, speech-to-text). Have a lawyer review the wording before it appears in the app or on the site. Don't call it a "guarantee" until then.
